### 4.3 The pause after `west_alone` (gap 10.4)

`west_alone` now stops East first, waits for East's valve to close (0 s every time, 72 of 72) and
then sets West. "West alone: learning paused after N s", over the catalogue, the repetitions and
BP12 on `2ca31af` (72 pauses):

| Pause after | Count | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 0 s | 37 | BP1, BP2, BP5, BP6 (first), BP10 ×22, BP11 ×11 |
| 10–60 s | 31 | BP3 (30), BP6 (second, 60), BP9 ×19 (50–60), BP12 ×10 (10–60) |
| 120 s | 4 | BP9 ×3, BP12 ×1 |

- 120 s, the old majority, is now 4 of 72 (5 of 73 with BP12's run on `c8bcc5d`).
- **0 s** comes from the plugin's rule (`handler._update_boiler_learning_pause`): the pause starts
  at the second consecutive calculation below the threshold, whatever the time between them. The
  runner's base reset (all setpoints to 20 °C) makes a calculation in which West calls and East does
  not; `west_alone`'s setpoint change makes the second. On instance 1 in BP1: West's base
  calculation at 19:59:33.444, East 20 → 7 °C at .731, West 20 → 23 °C and "learning paused" at
  .748, 304 ms later. Both readings were genuinely below the threshold. That is the design; it
  pauses on two calculations, not after a minimum time. In the check of `a2b0297` (BP12's first run,
  from HA's history, logged in decisions.log) VT counted East 4 ms after East's setpoint
  (17:03:09.810 → count 2 at .814), so 300 ms still leaves room, but no scenario tests two setpoint changes in
  quick succession at the boiler's start.
- After `load_learned_state` (BP9, BP12) the pause comes at West's next calculation, 10–60 s, or
  at the second, 120 s.
- BP5's 0.08× (pause 0 s, resume 10 s) and BP2's and BP10's 0.50–0.51× come from this: the typical
  time counts one calculation for the pause (gap 1). BP3's 0.50× is its pause after 30 s, the
  calculation's phase.

## 9. Gaps for the coding session

1. **Typical times after `west_alone` and after a window closes.**
   - The pause after `west_alone` is 0 s in 37 of 72, when the base reset's calculation already
     counts as the first below the threshold. BP5 runs at 0.08×, BP2, BP3 and BP10 at 0.50–0.51×.
     `PAUSE_ON_SETPOINT` could be typical 0 after `west_alone` from the base, or the base could
     leave West not calling.
   - BP11's "window closed: paused again" takes 150 s in 11 of 11 against `WINDOW_CLOSES`'s 90 s
     (30 s of VT's delay plus one calculation): West's first calculation after it heats again is not
     below the threshold yet, so the pause comes two calculations later.
2. **BP12 (b) needs its own reference without the delay.** The comparison with BR1 (§5.2) mixes two
   conditions. The same part (b) on a role without an activation delay (e.g. `boiler-count`) would
   give the like-for-like baseline for the owner's decision on BP12.
3. **Two setpoint changes in quick succession** at the boiler's start are not tested: the pause's
   confirmation is two calculations, 304 ms apart here (§4.3). VT counted East 4 ms after its
   setpoint in the check of `a2b0297`, so it holds today; a variant of BP8 with two quick changes would show it stays so.
5. **BP12 (a) after a repetition** reads the last outcome of the previous run's (b) ("detected" in
   runs 2–11), the same stale-outcome mechanism as §4.2. It is only noted in (a), so no verdict
   depends on it; resetting West's learning before (a), or naming the outcome "from before", would
   keep the note honest.
6. **Still open from the check of `a2b0297`:** 10.5 (detections attributable to a thermostat) and
   10.6 (`a` and `b` say nothing in BR1/BR2), by the owner's choice.
