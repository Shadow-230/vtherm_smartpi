`logs/2026-11-01-deadtime-detections-a2b0297.txt`; the scripts are in the session's scratchpad).

delayed by" (`dt = inflection_time - heat_start_time`, `deadtime_estimator.py:323–340`). The

## 9. The owner's decisions (plan §5)

1. **§2, BR1.**
   - `boiler-count`: qas's error is not larger than 0.5.2's and within 20 % at both points (+10 /
     +18 % against +203 / +114 %). By the rule the pause is useful.
   - `boiler-power`: qas's error is clearly smaller than 0.5.2's at both points (+7 / +21 % against
     +198 / +124 %). It is within 20 % at 48 h, but 0.8 point over at 96 h. The rule covers
     "within 20 % and not larger", "clearly smaller" and "larger"; it does not say what "clearly
     smaller but over 20 %" means.
   - My reading: the excess is the author's estimator on boiler-on episodes, the same ones 0.5.2
     measured with the same values (§6.3). The pause neither causes it nor could remove it, so for
     the pause the result counts as useful on both thresholds.
