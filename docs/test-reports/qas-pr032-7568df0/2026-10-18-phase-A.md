## 8. Findings for the smart-pi coding session

**SmartPI**

- **F1 — the diagnostics lag a change of the water source.** The diagnostic sensor writes when the
  VTherm climate's state changes, or on a forced update. The forced update is sent for
  intermediate publishes and learning changes (`sensor.py` `_update_from_climate` /
  `_async_force_update`; `handler.py` lines 945 and 1230), not when the water source changes
  (live / average / lasting). The `water` block can then lag SmartPI's state by up to one VTherm
  cycle. Seen in QW4: the repair `water_data_gone` at 21:25:56, while `water.source` was still
  published as `live` at the end of the scenario's wait. Suggested: force a diagnostics publish
  when the water source (and the boiler's call state) changes.
- **F2 — `emitter_cooling` not published within the pack's wait.** *(Corrected 2026-10-20.)* The
  scenarios wait at most grace + 135 s (1500 + 135 s) for the diagnostics to show
  `emitter_cooling`; the number they print ("1641 s after the call", "1661 s") is the time when
  that wait ended without seeing it, not the time SmartPI changed state — hence the near-constant
  value, which an earlier version of this report read as a steady offset. SmartPI's real
  transition time is not recorded. In phase C (learned state, 2026-10-20) QW5 saw it within the
  wait and passed, while QC2s failed again on the same wait. The grace is SmartPI's
  `BOILER_CALL_GRACE_S` (900 s) plus one cycle of a switched device (600 s). The most likely cause is F1 (the diagnostics lag SmartPI's state by up to one
  VTherm cycle); please check that the transition happens at the grace in `boiler_call.py` and
  that the pack records the time it is first seen.
