**No error from SmartPI in any log, in any phase.** Every SmartPI warning was provoked by a
scenario, or was a guard action logged at WARNING by the author's choice.

**What holds:**
- SmartPI learns a plain room to within 1.5 % of the truth, and `qas` learns the same model as 0.4.0.
- The upgrade from 0.4.0 to `qas` and the way back keep the learned model.
- Learned models survive restarts (QA5 compares them), reloads and option saves (shown by the logs and
  snapshots).
- The zone with two sources:
  - published its model after 19.5 h, within 0.5 % of the truth at its last check before a restart;
  - the floor follows the main source;
  - the floor's command over its water factor is right on a learned zone (40 of 40 samples);
  - the 15-minute fallback took over on time.
- The water inputs, the boiler's call, the providers and the repairs work.
- No form, repair or service failed because of SmartPI.

**What fails in SmartPI:**
- **F7 and F8, confirmed in phase B:** the exchange learning with neighbours.
  - F7: a door neighbour is never learned while an always-open neighbour that cannot be separated
    is configured.
  - F8: an always-open neighbour whose temperature moves on its own biases the zone's `a` and `b`
    by about −29 %, and SmartPI's `k` learning, which uses that `a`, then fails.
- **F1, confirmed in phases A and C:** the diagnostics are not written when the dependency layer's
  state changes (water source, door connection, source role). Most of the pack's timing FAILs and
  probably F2 come from it.
- **Candidates:**
  - F4: the central-boiler role learns `a` and `b` low;
  - F5: a slow floor on a valve learns `a` far too low;
  - F9: minor, AutoCalib after a learning reset.

**What the run itself got wrong, and corrected:**
- **F6 withdrawn.** QE3's room had been reset by the simulator, not cooled by the fallback.
- **F3 re-triaged.** The zone fit's drift after QE5's restart, and QE5's failed comparison, come
  from the pack's simulator resetting the rooms at a Home Assistant restart (P15).
- **F2 re-read.** "1641 s" is the end of the scenario's wait, not a SmartPI time.

**Test pack:** 17 defects (P1–P17), each with its change, in section 10.2.

## 5. What `qas` does well, what fails, what was not established

**Does well** (with the strongest evidence):
- **Learning a plain room:** QB1 gave `a` +0.7 % and `b` −1.5 % of the truth; QA5 kept them across
  two restarts.
- **Compatibility with 0.4.0 both ways:** QB2l–QB3, the same values to the sixth decimal.
- **The zone with two sources:**
  - the fit was published at 19.5 h, within 0.5 % at the last check before the restart (QE5);
  - the floor applies the main command (QE2: 27 samples, largest gap 0.0);
  - the water-factor division is right (QW6: 40 / 40);
  - the fallback took over 900 s after the lead's last command, at 100 % (QE3);
  - the form refuses every wrong master (QE1);
  - a changed master is warned at start (QE4).
- **Water and boiler:**
  - live water factors that match the water (QW1s, QW1);
  - hot water holds the command and the integral (QW2);
  - the boiler's call: held, the other reasons, warming (QW5, QC1, QC2s's other checks);
  - providers registered and unregistered (QW10);
  - repairs raised as specified (QW4's repair at 21:25:56).
- **Neighbours' reactions:** every window action applied (QD4); learning paused while a door is open
  and settling after it (QD5s, QD5: no dead time learned in 9 openings).
- **Robustness:** no SmartPI error in about 100 scenario runs. PR #1 (AutoCalib's `entity_id`) and
  PR #14 (no false master warning, no duplicate sensor) hold on a real VTherm.

**Fails:**
- **F7, F8: the exchange learning with neighbours** (section 8.5).
- **F1: the diagnostics' writes.**
- **F4, F5, candidates:** learning `a` with a boiler not seen as water, and with a slow floor.

**Not established**, with what would establish it in section 10.4:
- QD2's reset;
- QE6's shedding;
- QA7;
- QE3's room criterion on a learned zone;
- F2's real transition time;
- forms on a learned system, beyond QA2, QE1, QE2, QW9;
- the Smart Boiler set, which was never in scope.

## 6. The 0.4.0 reference

runs 0.4.0.

| Does `qas` learn and regulate no worse than 0.4.0? | QB1s (45 min), QB1 (48 h, `compare.py qb1`) | **yes**. The same model: `qas` `a` 0.035261 / `b` 0.001182, 0.4.0 `a` 0.035142 / `b` 0.001181, truth 0.035 / 0.0012. Mean signed error (setpoint − room) 24–48 h: `qas` −0.063 °C, 0.4.0 −0.019 °C, so `qas` holds the room 0.044 °C warmer. | Does the upgrade keep 0.4.0's learned model? | QB2l → deploy `qas` → QB2 | **yes**. `a`, `b` and both update counts unchanged (0.035142, 0.001181, 9, 5); no warning about the saved format; the migrated entry keeps its own values; the old options open and save. **A note for going back and forth.** Saving the options in 0.4.0 keeps the fork's keys. After the
save, `smart_pi_dependencies` still held the attic neighbour that QB3a had added. 0.4.0 ignores
them, but a later return to `qas` would find that neighbour configured again. QB3's text says
both that 0.4.0 drops the keys and that the base is restored; both are untrue for the stored
options.

## 7. Coverage and limits

- **All 47 scenarios ran at least once.** The minimum sets ran on a fresh state (A) and again on a
  learned state (C), except the six left out of C for their pack defects (QA1, QA7, QD2, QD3,
  QW3, QW4) and three not in C's plan (QD1, QB1s, QE5s). The long scenarios ran once each (B).
- **One simulator, on one bench.** The plant is the pack's linear model: no solar gain, no
  occupants, sensors to 0.1 °C. Each long run ran once, so there is no spread of learned values.
- **Two sets were out of scope:** Smart Boiler, which waits for the owner's decision on how to test
  it, and instances beyond 8. The owner's home installation was never contacted.
- **The pack's own defects limit what some FAILs say** (section 4.2). The simulator's reset at a
  Home Assistant restart (P15) touched exactly QE5 (B) and QE3 (C).
- **0.4.0 was compared in the `base` role only.**

### 8.5 The neighbour flag "indistinguishable" — where it comes from, and whether it should be there (2026-10-20 14:45; final stores 21:10 and 03:45)

**Question:** "indistinguishable" may or may not be a good sign; analyse where it comes from in
each case, to understand whether it should be there.

**Method:**
1. The kitchen's saved coupling state was copied read-only from instances 3 and 1, at 2026-10-20
   14:19 and again after each run ended (`indist/instance3-kitchen-store-final.json`,
   `indist/instance1-kitchen-store-final.json`).
2. It was recomputed offline with SmartPI's own `coupling_estimator.py` of 7568df0 (`indist/spk/`),
   using `indist/joint.py`, `door.py` and `reduced.py`.
3. **Counterfactual:** the 30-min windows were rebuilt from the recorder like SmartPI builds them:
   - a 60 s tick;
   - a new window when the connected set changes, and none during the 10 min after a door change;
   - SmartPI's slope weights, the power delayed by the dead time, and its 0.5 °C informative test.

   The windows were then fitted twice, with the simulator's true model and with SmartPI's
   (`indist/history_fetch.py`, `counterfactual.py`, `a_scan.py`, `a_scan_no_neighbour.py`).
4. **Validation of the rebuild:** with the full true model it recovers the hall's `k` as 0.00395
   (true 0.004), and leaves a residual of ~0.0005 °C/min, the floor that the 0.1 °C sensor allows.

**How SmartPI sets the flag** (`smartpi/coupling_estimator.py`):
- **Routing:** a window that contains any always-connected neighbour goes to the joint fit
  (`_submit`, 583–588). This applies to the hall too while the door is open, so the door's own
  path (589–595) is never reached.
- **All or nothing:** the joint fit (`_solve_joint_rows`, 620–634) is refused if the regressors
  cannot be told apart (condition number > 30 or variation < 0.5 °C), or if a coefficient's
  relative error exceeds 0.15.
- **When it is refused,** every item in it is flagged (`is_indistinguishable`, 390–394).

**The three cases:**

| Case | Where the flag comes from | Counterfactual | Should it be there? |
|---|---|---|---|
| QD5, dining (always open, held at its setpoint) | the columns dining − outdoor and outdoor − kitchen correlate −0.998, condition number 6367 | refused **with the true model too** | **yes**: a limit of the data. Its exchange is a near-constant offset already in SmartPI's `b` (0.001127 against 0.001); the kitchen's model is right (`a` 0.0356) |
| QD5, hall (door) | its own column barely correlates (−0.17 with the dining's, +0.22 with outdoor − kitchen); it is flagged only because it shares the joint fit the dining makes fail | the best-determined coefficient with the true model (0.00397 ± 0.00009); separable with the dining folded into the loss: 0.00369 true model, 0.00373 SmartPI's model; SmartPI's own 90 windows refitted that way 0.00336 ± 0.00016, through the door path 0.00325 | **no**: F7 |

### 8.6 Telling a hidden model error from the model, and how to correct it in the plugin (2026-10-20 15:05; QD6's counterfactual 2026-10-21 03:45)

**Question:** consider an approach that tells better what hides a model error and what is the
model, and how to correct it within the plugin's code.

**The approach:**
1. **Counterfactual on the same data.** Fit the same windows with the true model and with SmartPI's.
   - Separable with the true model but not with SmartPI's → SmartPI's model is wrong.
   - Separable with neither → a limit of the data.
2. **Residual against the sensor's noise.** Compare the spread of SmartPI's own model's residual
   with the noise floor of the windows.
3. **Data-driven `a`.** Find the `a` that fits the data best.
4. **The omitted term.** Fit the zone's model without the neighbour's term, as SmartPI has it while
   `k` is unknown, and see whether that alone reproduces SmartPI's bias.

**The numbers:**

| Test | QD5 (instance 3) | QD6 (instance 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Residual of SmartPI's model against the floor | 0.00094 °C/min = 1.8 × the floor (part of it the dining's exchange kept in `b`) | 0.00302 °C/min = **6.2 ×** the floor |
| `a` that best fits the data | — | **0.035**, the truth: residual 0.00049; at SmartPI's 0.0249 it is 2.3 × larger |
| `a`, `b`, `k` fitted together | — | `a` 0.03524, `b` 0.001006, `k` 0.00301: the truth |
| `a`, `b` fitted without the neighbour's term | on the 71 door-closed windows: `a` 0.0348, `b` 0.0011, right, the dining is steady | `a` 0.0157, `b` 0.00046: biased low, the direction of SmartPI's 0.0249 and 0.000716 |

**The mechanism of F8.** SmartPI leaves an always-open neighbour of unknown `k` out of its model,
and counts on its own `b` to hold that exchange (`dependency_model.py` 415–418).
- **A steady neighbour** (QD5) is held by `b`.
- **A neighbour that moves on its own** (QD6) biases `a` and `b` low: an omitted-variable bias.
- **The two learnings wait on each other.** SmartPI's `k` learning uses SmartPI's `a`
  (`coupling_estimator.py` `_submit` 582), and its `a` learning has no term for a neighbour whose `k`
  is unknown. A biased `a` gives either a refused fit or a biased `k`, and nothing corrects `a`
  while `k` is unknown.
- **Why SmartPI's own fit was refused is not established.** The rebuilt windows with SmartPI's final
  `a` would pass the error test and publish `k` 31 % low; SmartPI's own 85 windows are noisier
  (relative 0.44). The challenge's rough rebuild with a piecewise `a` gives 0.12, so the changing `a`
  explains only part of it.

**The corrections proposed for the plugin** are the To-fix items F7 and F8 (section 10.1) and the
diagnostics' reason:

| Reason | Meaning | Today's cases |
|---|---|---|
| `collinear` | the regressors cannot be told apart: a real limit | QD5's dining |
| `uncertain` | separable, but the relative error is too large | QD6's dining |
| `implausible` | negative, or above the maximum | — |
| `blocked` | its own column is separable, but another item made the fit fail | QD5's hall |
| `shared` | doors' common coefficient | — |

Each reason comes with the condition number, the relative error and the number of windows.
`indistinguishable` stays true only for `collinear` and `shared`. A residual-against-noise check
then says "the zone's model does not fit" instead of "uncertain".

**Open point:** SmartPI's own saved windows are noisier than the rebuild with its final parameters
(relative error 0.44 against 0.04 on instance 1). On instance 3 they give the hall 0.0033 against
0.0037. The likely causes are `a` changing during the run, and the power SmartPI counts per cycle,
the temperature it is fed, and which windows it keeps (90 against 107).

### 11.1 A door-open setpoint action

**The idea** (the owner, 2026-10-20 15:30): neighbouring rooms can have different setpoints, for
example 20 and 21 °C. SmartPI could offer an option: if the door has been open longer than a set
time, raise the setpoint to the highest, lower it to the lowest, do nothing, and so on.

**The owner's decisions** (2026-10-20 15:40):
- the user chooses the action per door neighbour, when setting up the dependencies, as for the
  window action;
- SmartPI recommends nothing;
- the item goes on the list to be specified with the smart-pi session.

**What the code has today** (read in 7568df0):
- **A door neighbour** only pauses learning while open (`dependency_model.py` `_gate`,
  `GATE_DOOR_OPEN`, then `door_settling`). Once its `k` is known it enters the model as a known
  exchange. There is no setpoint action.
- **An open window at a thermostat neighbour already has an action:** nothing / `turn_off` /
  `frost` / `eco` (`smartpi/dependencies.py` `WINDOW_ACTIONS` 28–29).
  - It is chosen in `dependency_model._window_action` (985–1010) and applied in
    `algo._apply_neighbour_window_action` (2058–2087).
  - SmartPI imposes its own target internally, without changing Versatile Thermostat's setpoint, and
    never one needing more energy than the current setpoint.
  - The form shows it only for thermostat neighbours (`config_flow.py` 1003).

The door action can reuse that path.

**Proposed shape:**
- Per door neighbour that is a thermostat:
  - `door_open_action`: `nothing` (the default, today's behaviour), `highest` (the higher of the
    zone's and the neighbours' setpoints) or `lowest` (the lower);
  - `door_open_delay` in minutes: the action starts only after the door has been open that long, and
    ends when it closes.
- With several doors open, the highest or the lowest over all of them.
- An open window at a neighbour takes priority over the door action.

**Missing pieces in the code:**
- `NeighbourReading` (`smartpi/dependency_inputs.py` 38–97) does not read the neighbour's setpoint:
  add the climate's `temperature` attribute;
- `_track_doors` keeps only the door's state: add the time it opened;
- the form: the two fields, for thermostat neighbours only;
- diagnostics: the active action, the imposed setpoint and which neighbour set it.

**Points to specify:**
1. `highest` needs more energy, the opposite of the window action's rule; the documentation must
   say so.
2. Opposite options on the two sides of one door would swap the setpoints (20 °C with `highest`,
   21 °C with `lowest` give 21 and 20). The form could warn, or recommend the same option on both
   sides.
3. Compare with the neighbour's own (user) setpoint, not with one its SmartPI overrides.
4. Cooling mode: what `highest` and `lowest` mean there.
5. Whether the override bypasses the setpoint boost / trajectory, as the window action does.
6. The window action's priority, and an open window at the zone itself.

Learning is already paused while the door is open, so the override does not disturb the model.

**Tests once built:**
- in-process: each option, the delay (shorter and longer openings), several doors, the window's
  priority, the return on close;
- QAS: a scenario in the `neighbours` role, with the kitchen and the hall at different setpoints
  and each option.
